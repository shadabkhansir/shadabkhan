Case Studies

Problems I've solved

Four representative projects, each told the same way: the problem, what I did, what changed.

Retention · Ecommerce

Rebuilding retention across 6 GCC markets

Problem

Five storefronts, six markets, one generic blast calendar. Repeat purchase rates were flat and CRM revenue was under-attributed because tracking was unreliable.

Approach

Re-architected segmentation in CleverTap around behaviour and purchase recency, replaced calendar blasts with behavioral-trigger journeys (onboarding, replenishment, win-back, cart recovery), and localised messaging per market across email, SMS, push and in-app.

Outcome

Repeat purchase rate up 40%; CRM-attributed revenue up 30% across 5 storefronts. Win-back journeys reactivated 12% of lapsed customers within 90 days.

Analytics · Attribution

Attribution you can trust: the GA4 + GTM rebuild

Problem

Lifecycle decisions were being made on dirty data — broken events, duplicate transactions, and CRM revenue that couldn't be traced to specific journeys. Nobody fully trusted the dashboards.

Approach

Audited the full event taxonomy, rebuilt the GTM container with a clean data-layer spec, aligned GA4 events with CRM platform events, and partnered with ecommerce and analytics teams on ongoing data-hygiene ownership.

Outcome

Clean attribution became the foundation for the 40% repeat-purchase result above. Event tracking accuracy reached 95%+, and monthly reporting time dropped by 60%.

Lifecycle · B2B Growth

The organic growth engine behind a 3x revenue year

Problem

A deep-tech startup with a long, consultative sales cycle and a lead pipeline almost entirely dependent on paid spend and founder networks.

Approach

Built a compounding organic engine: SEO and content targeting high-intent queries, website conversion optimisation, and an email newsletter that kept the long-cycle audience warm — all wired into CRM nurture flows that scored and progressed leads automatically.

Outcome

Organic lead generation up 80%, contributing to 3x company revenue growth within one year. Organic share of pipeline grew from 15% to 45%.

Automation · Demand Gen

1,000+ leads from launch — and a CRM that could handle them

Problem

A Product Hunt launch was about to flood the funnel, but lead handling was manual: no scoring, no lifecycle stages, and sales handoff by spreadsheet.

Approach

Automated the full HubSpot lifecycle ahead of launch — capture forms, enrichment, lead scoring, nurture sequences and lifecycle-stage progression — then ran the launch and surrounding demand-gen campaigns.

Outcome

1,000+ inbound leads captured, scored and routed with zero manual triage, contributing to 35% growth in new customer acquisition. Sales handoff time dropped from days to under 1 hour.
