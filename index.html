Case Studies

Percentages are relative lifts. Absolute revenue figures are kept confidential.

Challenge
When I took over CRM, there were 56 live customer journeys and together they converted at under 1%. The journeys were running across five storefronts and six markets, but they weren't bringing customers back.
What I found
I went through every journey one by one. The biggest problem was the Welcome journey, our main entry point. A wrongly set goal event was sending tens of thousands of new customers into a dead end. I also found that app and in-app messages converted about 10× better than web.
What I did
I cut the 56 journeys down to a clean framework of 17, covering onboarding, repeat purchase, loyalty, win-back and cart recovery. I fixed the Welcome journey, moved effort toward the channels that actually converted, and added back-in-stock and price-drop triggers so customers heard from us when they had a real reason to buy. Everything runs in CleverTap across email, SMS, push and in-app, for brands including GHD, BaByliss Pro and Revlon.
Impact
  • +40%repeat purchase rate
  • +30%CRM-attributed revenue
  • 12×Welcome journey conversion
Measured across five GCC storefronts.
Challenge
Six regional sites shared one app, and each one tracked things differently. Attribution had gaps, and some of the data flows weren't compliant. It was hard to say which campaigns were really driving revenue.
What I did
I wrote a 32-event GA4 and GTM specification and set up server-side routing to GA4, Google Ads, Meta CAPI and TikTok CAPI. Hashed customer data goes only to Enhanced Conversions and the CAPIs, and GA4 receives no personal data at all. I then worked with the performance, e-commerce and analytics teams to keep event tracking and customer data clean.
Impact
  • 32events in one shared spec
  • 6markets on the same tracking
Consent-aware attribution across every market. This is the data the retention results in CS·01 are measured on, so the team knows which journeys actually earn their revenue.
Challenge
We sold the same products on our own site and on Amazon and Noon. Our own site was losing out, and products running out of stock were quietly costing us sales.
What I did
I compared SKU performance across all three channels to find exactly where a discount would win sales back to our own site. I built an out-of-stock model to put a number on lost revenue. And I set a rule that every campaign checks live stock before it sends, so we never promote a hero product that people can't buy.
Impact
A clear figure for the revenue lost to stock gaps, plus a targeted discount list to bring volume back to our own site. CRM decisions now account for stock and margin, and campaigns no longer drive customers to empty shelves.
Challenge
The business was expanding into four new GCC markets. Each storefront had to be ready to sell, and ready to bring customers back, from launch.
What I did
I planned the regional launch, built a formula-based media plan across the four markets, and ran a competitor analysis to find gaps we could own. I produced the launch video with AI tools and worked with our development partner to get the stores live.
Impact
The four new stores joined the same tracking and lifecycle setup as the rest of the business, so every market was measurable from the start. [ add launch results ]
