Case Studies

All numbers indexed / anonymised. Employer referenced as “a leading GCC beauty & cosmetics retailer.”

Problem
Inherited 56 live lifecycle journeys converting under 1% overall.
Diagnosis
Audited every journey. Found the flagship Welcome journey pulling tens of thousands of users into a dead end through a mis-configured goal event — and app/in-app surfaces converting roughly 10× better than web.
Built
A canonical 17-journey lifecycle framework spanning acquisition → retention → value growth. Fixed the goal-event logic, reallocated effort to the high-converting surfaces, and stood up back-in-stock and price-drop trigger journeys.
Impact
Flagship journey conversion improved [ 12× ]; top performer (wishlist abandonment) sustained [ ~18% conversion ].
Next
[ Holdout-based incrementality measurement ]
Problem
Fragmented, non-compliant tracking across six regional sites sharing one app.
Built
A 32-event GA4/GTM specification with server-side routing to GA4, Google Ads, Meta CAPI and TikTok CAPI — plus a PII-compliance split: hashed user data for Enhanced Conversions and CAPI, kept apart from non-PII GA4 properties. GDPR / consent-aware by design.
Impact
[ Clean attribution · compliant data flow · conversion-accuracy improvement ]
Why it matters
This is the data layer most retention managers can't touch — and the compliance rigour Western markets require.
Problem
The same SKUs sold on our own site and on marketplaces (Amazon, Noon); own-site underperforming, with heavy stock gaps leaking revenue.
Built
A cross-channel SKU analysis pinpointing precise discount targets to lift own-site sales; an out-of-stock / opportunity-loss model; and a standing rule that every campaign runs a live stock check — out-of-stock heroes never get pushed.
Impact
Identified [ seven-figure, indexed ] revenue-at-risk from stock gaps, plus a targeted discount list to recover own-site volume.
Why it matters
Retention thinking tied directly to margin and merchandising — not just messaging.
Problem
Stand up e-commerce and engagement for four new GCC storefronts, from zero.
Built
Regional launch strategy, a formula-driven media plan across all four markets, competitive white-space analysis, AI-produced launch video creative, and cross-functional delivery with an external dev vendor.
